Privacy Notice

HapIsabela uses the information you provide to operate your account, publish vacancies, and process job applications.

Account and application information

Your account may contain your name, email, phone number, location, company name, and profile description. Applications include your message and the documents you choose to upload. Supabase provides authentication, database storage, and private document storage.

Who can see your information

Administrators manage accounts and applications. Employers can access applications submitted to their own vacancies. Approved company names, business descriptions, locations, and job openings may appear publicly. Application documents use restricted access and temporary viewing links.

Your choices

You can update your profile and password in Profile settings. Contact your portal administrator for requests concerning your account, submitted applications, or stored documents. Avoid uploading information unrelated to a vacancy.
