Terms of Use

Using your account

Provide accurate information, keep your password private, and use the portal for legitimate job searches or recruitment. Public registration is available to job seekers and companies. Company accounts need administrator approval before posting jobs. Administrator accounts are assigned by authorized administrators.

Jobs and applications

Employers are responsible for accurate vacancy details. Submit only documents you are entitled to share. Do not post misleading vacancies, request applicant fees, impersonate others, or misuse personal information. Administrators can moderate listings and suspend accounts.

Verify opportunities

HapIsabela is an independent employment portal. Portal approval does not constitute government accreditation or guarantee a job offer. Verify employers and employment terms independently, and never pay to submit an application here.

Getting help

Contact your portal administrator for account questions or concerns about a vacancy or application.
